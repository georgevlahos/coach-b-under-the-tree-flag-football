## Learning path

We teach in this order:

1. **Routes** — Wide receivers learn the routes they will run (START HERE — see `routes.md`)
2. **Formations** — Where everyone lines up (Spread, Trips Left, Trips Right)
3. **Plays** — Route combinations called from each formation (coming later)

Most girls will play **wide receiver** first. Focus on filling in the **Wide Receiver** sections below, and the full route tree in `routes.md`.

## Example — Wide Receiver X

### Wide Receiver X — `X`

**Short name:** X
**Emoji:** ⭐

**Where do you line up?**
Far left — outside receiver. In Spread you're the leftmost. In Trips Left you're the outside of the left bunch. In Trips Right you're alone on the left.

**What is your job?**
Run your route every play, catch the ball if it comes to you, and be ready even when you're just clearing space for a teammate.

**Routes you run:**

| Formation   | Route(s) you run |
| ----------- | ---------------- |
| Spread      | Slant, Go        |
| Trips Left  | Out              |
| Trips Right | Go (clear out)   |

**How to run your routes:**
- **Slant:** 3 steps upfield, plant and cut inside at 45°. Eyes on QB!
- **Go:** Run straight as fast as you can — beat your defender deep.
- **Out:** 5 steps up, plant outside foot, break hard to the sideline.

**Coach tip:**
Sell the fake! If you're running a Go, look like you're going deep from the first step.

**How Coach B describes it:**
"X, you're our left weapon — know your route before the snap!"

**Quiz ideas:**
- What route does X run in Spread?
- Describe the Slant route in your own words.
