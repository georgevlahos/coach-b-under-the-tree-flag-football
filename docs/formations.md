## How to read the diagram

Every formation uses the same **7 positions**. Left/right is from the **offense's perspective** (QB facing the end zone):

| Spot on field        | ID   | Role                          |
| -------------------- | ---- | ----------------------------- |
| Leftmost circle      | `X`  | Wide receiver — left outside  |
| Second from left     | `L`  | Wide receiver — left slot     |
| Top of center group  | `C`  | Center (snaps the ball)       |
| Bottom-left of group | `H`  | Halfback                      |
| Bottom-right of group| `QB` | Quarterback                   |
| Second from right    | `R`  | Wide receiver — right slot    |
| Rightmost circle     | `Z`  | Wide receiver — right outside |

The center group (C, H, QB) forms a **triangle** — C on top at the line, H offset left in the backfield, QB offset right in the backfield.

## Spread — `spread`

```
                    END ZONE
    ← LEFT SIDELINE          RIGHT SIDELINE →

    (X)----(L)----(C)----(R)----(Z)
                  \       /
                   (H)  (QB)
```

**What it looks like:**
Receivers spread evenly — two on the left (X, L), two on the right (R, Z), with the center triangle in the middle.

**Alignment (from playbook):**
- **X:** Far left, outside receiver
- **L:** Second from left (left slot) — labeled on wristband
- **C:** Center, top of triangle, on the line
- **H:** Just off center left, backfield
- **QB:** Bottom right of triangle, shotgun
- **R:** Second from right (right slot)
- **Z:** Far right, outside receiver

**What each receiver runs:**

| ID | Alignment in Spread | Route(s) |
| -- | ------------------- | -------- |
| X  | Far left            |          |
| L  | Left slot           |          |
| R  | Right slot          |          |
| Z  | Far right           |          |

**Coach B call:**

**When we use it:**

**5th grade tip:**
Everyone spreads out — two on each side like stretching a rubber band!

**Quiz ideas:**
- In Spread, who is the leftmost receiver?
- Where does the L line up in Spread?

## Trips Left — `trips-left`

```
                    END ZONE

    (X)--(L)--(R)----(C)----------------(Z)
                      \       /
                       (H)  (QB)
```

**What it looks like:**
Three receivers **bunched on the left** (X, L, R). Z alone on the far right. Same center triangle.

**Alignment (from playbook):**
- **X:** Far left, outside of the trips bunch
- **L:** Middle of the left bunch (labeled on wristband)
- **R:** Innermost of the left bunch, closest to center
- **C:** Center, top of triangle
- **H:** Just off center left, backfield
- **QB:** Bottom right of triangle, shotgun
- **Z:** Far right, alone (isolated receiver)

**What each receiver runs:**

| ID | Alignment in Trips Left | Route(s) |
| -- | ----------------------- | -------- |
| X  | Far left (outside trips)|          |
| L  | Middle of left bunch  |          |
| R  | Inside of left bunch  |          |
| Z  | Far right (isolated)  |          |

**Coach B call:**

**When we use it:**

**5th grade tip:**
Three friends on the left, Z all alone on the right — someone has to cover Z!

**Quiz ideas:**
- How many receivers are on the left in Trips Left?
- Who is the isolated receiver on the right?

## Trips Right — `trips-right`

```
                    END ZONE

    (X)----------------(C)----(L)--(R)--(Z)
                          \       /
                           (H)  (QB)
```

**What it looks like:**
X alone on the far left. Three receivers **bunched on the right** (L, R, Z). Same center triangle.

**Alignment (from playbook):**
- **X:** Far left, alone (isolated receiver)
- **C:** Center, top of triangle
- **H:** Just off center left, backfield
- **QB:** Bottom right of triangle, shotgun
- **L:** Innermost of the right bunch, closest to center (labeled on wristband)
- **R:** Middle of the right bunch
- **Z:** Far right, outside of the trips bunch

**What each receiver runs:**

| ID | Alignment in Trips Right | Route(s) |
| -- | ------------------------ | -------- |
| X  | Far left (isolated)    |          |
| L  | Inside of right bunch  |          |
| R  | Middle of right bunch  |          |
| Z  | Far right (outside trips)|        |

**Coach B call:**

**When we use it:**

**5th grade tip:**
Mirror of Trips Left — now three friends stack up on the right!

**Quiz ideas:**
- Who is alone on the left in Trips Right?
- What's the difference between Trips Left and Trips Right?
